Our Core Services

Design & Engineering

Custom CAD designs, site assessments and international compliance — optimised for space efficiency, cost-effectiveness and self-supported dome structures.

Manufacturing & Supply

Zincalume® steel panels, GI powder-coated & epoxy-coated materials, factory-made membrane liners and hot-dip galvanized fittings — shipped with full documentation.

Installation & Erection

On-site assembly, technical supervision, quality control inspections and hydrostatic testing before handover — ensuring leak-free operation and zero project delays.

Tank Inspection & Maintenance

Annual corrosion checks, potable tank cleaning, preventive maintenance planning and component replacement — liners, bolts and gaskets — to extend system lifespan.

Custom Fabrication & Retrofitting

Tailor-made solutions for unusual dimensions, retrofitting existing tanks with new liners or roofs, and capacity upgrades using modular systems with minimal structural changes.

Precision Rolling & Punching

High-precision sheet rolling for curved panels and dome structures, plus advanced punching for bolt holes, nozzle openings and truck body components.

Built to International Standards

Every Al Karwan tank is manufactured and installed in strict compliance with internationally recognised engineering, material, and safety standards — ensuring long-term performance across the UAE's demanding climate.

Zincalume® Steel Panels (AS 1397)

Zinc-aluminium alloy coated corrugated steel panels offering superior corrosion resistance and structural integrity.

Food-Grade PVC Liner (NSF/ANSI 61)

Potable-water-certified liner system that prevents contamination and ensures hygienic water storage from day one.

Hot-Dip Galvanized Hardware (ASTM A123)

All bolts, nozzles, and fittings are galvanized to ASTM A123 — rust-proof and engineered for decades of reliable service.

GI Powder-Coated & Epoxy Options

Galvanized iron tanks available with powder-coat or epoxy finish for enhanced weather resistance and aesthetic appeal.
